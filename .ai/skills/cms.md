# CMS & Publications Management Skill

> **Platform**: UEW Innovation Hub Enterprise  
> **Domain**: Publications, Research Papers, and Newsletters

---

## 1. Capabilities
- Manage research papers, policy briefs, and innovation newsletter issues.
- Automatic thumbnail and cover preview generation.
- Categorization by thematic area (STEM, Educational Tech, Agricultural Innovation, Inclusive Education).
- Tagging and cross-linking to funded projects.
- Public read-only REST API endpoint (`/api/v1/publications`) for embedding on main UEW institutional portal.
- Secure direct PDF streaming for registered researchers and accredited partners.
