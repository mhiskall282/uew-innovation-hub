# Badges and leaderboard

> **Audience:** Members and the public · **Last verified:** 2026-11-30 against commit `9f3bdee`
> **Related:** [Profile and account management](../profile-and-account-management.md), [Programme applications](programme-applications.md), [Progress reports](progress-reports.md), [Business logic](../../04-architecture/business-logic.md)

This page explains experience points (XP), levels, badges and the public community page. It shows how to earn XP, how to appear on the leaderboard and how to leave it.

- **What it does:** rewards taking part and recognises results such as winning a competition.
- **Why:** to encourage members to apply, report and take part. It has no effect on whether an application is accepted.
- **Who:** every member earns XP. Only members who opt in appear in public. Staff award most badges.
- **Where:** your level and badges are on **My account** (`/portal`), in the card **Your level**. The public page is **Community & leaderboard** in the footer, at `/community`.

## How you earn XP

| Action | XP | Who triggers it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Submit an application | +10 | You |
| Submit a new progress report | +20 | You. Resubmitting an edited report adds nothing |
| Accepted into a programme (Participant badge) | +50 | Automatic when a reviewer sets the status to Accepted |
| Alumni badge | +100 | Staff |
| Mentor badge | +120 | Staff |
| Finalist badge | +150 | Staff |
| Winner badge | +300 | Staff |
| Recognition (custom) badge | +50 by default | Staff. Staff can set a different amount from 0 to 1,000 |

The same badge type for the same programme is only awarded once, with no extra XP. If staff remove a badge, its XP is taken away, never below zero. XP cannot go down in any other way. Withdrawing an application does not remove the 10 XP you earned for submitting it.

You get a notification, "You earned a badge: <title>", with the XP amount.

## Levels

| Level | XP needed |
| --- | --- |
| Newcomer | 0 |
| Explorer | 50 |
| Builder | 150 |
| Innovator | 400 |
| Trailblazer | 800 |
| Hub Legend | 1,500 |

The **Your level** card on My account shows your level name, XP, a progress bar and "N XP to <next level>". At the top level it says "Highest level reached".

## Badge types

| Badge | Icon | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Winner | Trophy | Won a competition or award |
| Finalist | Trophy | Reached the final |
| Participant | Star | Accepted into a programme. For an incubation programme the badge title is "Incubatee: <programme>", otherwise "Selected: <programme>" |
| Alumni | Academic cap | Completed a programme |
| Mentor | People | Mentors others |
| Recognition | Star | Any other recognition from staff |

My account shows up to 6 badges. Your public profile shows all of them.

## The public community page

`/community` shows:

- **Leaderboard:** up to 30 members, highest XP first, with affiliation, institution, XP and badge count.
- **Recent badges:** the latest 8 badges awarded to members on the leaderboard.
- **How XP works:** a short table of the rules.
- If nobody qualifies yet: "The leaderboard is waiting for its first names".

Press a name to open the member's public profile, `/community/<number>`. It shows name, affiliation, institution, bio, XP, rank and badges.

### Who is listed

A member appears only when **all three** are true:

1. The account is a member and active.
2. **Show me on the public community page and leaderboard** is ticked in **Profile & settings**.
3. XP is more than 0.

Your e-mail address, phone number, region, gender, age and other personal details are never shown.

### Join or leave

1. Open **Profile & settings** (`/portal/profile`).
2. Under **Communication**, tick or untick **Show me on the public community page and leaderboard**.
3. Press **Save profile**.

Expected result: the change applies at once. If you untick it, your public profile address returns "Page not found" and you disappear from the list.

You can also use the link in the **Your level** card: **Appear on the community leaderboard** (opens your settings) or **View my public profile**.

## Errors and fixes

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I am not on the leaderboard | Public profile is off, or you have 0 XP, or you are outside the top 30 | Tick the box. Submit an application to earn XP |
| My rank is not shown | Rank is only shown when XP is above 0 | Earn XP |
| A badge is missing | Staff award badges by hand, except Participant | Contact the Hub and quote your application reference |
| My XP went down | Staff removed a badge | Contact the Hub |
| XP did not change after I submitted | You saved a draft only. XP is for submitted applications | Check the status on **My applications** |
| Public profile gives "Page not found" | The profile is private, or the account is inactive | Switch the setting on |
