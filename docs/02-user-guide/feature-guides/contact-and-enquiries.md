# Contact and enquiries

> **Audience:** Everyone, no account needed · **Last verified:** 2026-11-30 against commit `9f3bdee`
> **Related:** [Application messages](application-messages.md), [Common user issues](../common-user-issues.md), [User FAQ](../user-faq.md), [Content management](../../03-administration/content-management.md)

This page explains the contact form, what happens to your message, and which route to use for different questions.

- **What it does:** sends a message to the Hub team.
- **Why:** for questions about projects, partnerships, funding, media or technical problems that are not about one application.
- **Who:** anyone. You do not need an account.
- **Where:** **Contact** in the top menu, **Contact us** in the footer, or `/contact`.

## Choose the right route

| Your question | Use |
| --- | --- |
| About one application you made | [Application messages](application-messages.md). It stays with the application |
| I cannot sign in or do not get a code | The contact form, or [Common user issues](../common-user-issues.md) first |
| Partnerships, funding, media, general questions | The contact form |
| Something on the site is wrong | The contact form, with the address of the page |

## Send a message

1. Open `/contact`. The left side shows the Hub's address, e-mail, phone (when set), office hours and social links.
2. Fill in the form **Send us a message**.
3. Press **Send message**.

| Field | Rule | Message if wrong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your name | Required, up to 255 characters | "The name field is required." |
| Email address | Required, valid e-mail, up to 255 characters | "The email field must be a valid email address." |
| Phone (optional) | 7 to 30 characters: digits, spaces, `+ ( ) -` | "Enter a valid phone number." |
| Subject | Required, up to 200 characters | "The subject field is required." |
| Message | Required, 10 to 5,000 characters | "The message field must be at least 10 characters." |

Expected result: the page shows a green panel, "Message sent. Thank you for contacting the UEW Innovation Hub. A member of the team will reply by email." Press **Send another message** to write a new one.

The page says "We usually reply within two working days." This is the Hub's intention. The site does not measure it.

The privacy line under the form says your details are used only to reply to the message, in line with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

## What happens to your message

1. The message is stored with your name, e-mail, phone, subject, text and the internet address it came from.
2. The Hub records it in the audit log as "Enquiry from <name>".
3. Staff who may read enquiries (Programme Officer and Hub Director) see a number badge next to **Enquiries** in the workspace. They can also get an e-mail if they switched on **New contact-form enquiries** in their account settings.
4. A staff member opens it, which marks it as read, and replies from their own e-mail to the address you gave.

You do not get an automatic confirmation e-mail. The green panel is your confirmation.

## Limits and spam protection

- You can send 3 messages a minute and 20 a day from one internet connection. After that you see the "Too many requests" page. Wait a minute.
- The form has a hidden field called "Leave this field empty". Automated programs fill it in. If it is filled, the site shows "Your message could not be sent."

## Errors and fixes

| What you see | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Please check the highlighted fields." | A field failed a rule | Read the red text under each field |
| "Enter a valid phone number." | Letters in the phone box | Remove them, or leave it empty |
| "Your message could not be sent." | The hidden spam field was filled, often by browser auto-fill tools | Turn off form-filling extensions and try again |
| "Too many requests" (429) | Over 3 per minute or 20 per day | Wait, then send one message |
| "This page expired" (419) | The page was open too long | Refresh and send again |
| No reply after two working days | The message may be in the staff queue, or your e-mail address was typed wrongly | Check your spam folder. Send again with the correct address |
