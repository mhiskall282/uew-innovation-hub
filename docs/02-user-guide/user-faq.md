Short answers to common questions, grouped by topic. Each answer links to the page with the full steps. For error messages, see [Common user issues](common-user-issues.md).

## Accounts

**Do I need an account to read the site?**
No. Programmes, projects, publications, news, gallery, partners and contact are public. You need an account to apply. See [Getting started](getting-started.md).

**Who can create an account?**
Anyone aged 15 or over. Students, staff, alumni, partner organisations and the public are all welcome. The affiliation is chosen on the profile page.

**Why does the form ask for gender, date of birth and region?**
For eligibility checks on targeted programmes and for anonymous reporting to funders. Reviewers do not see your gender. See [Account registration](account-registration.md).

**Is the sign-up open?**
Usually. The Hub Director can pause it, and then the page says "New registrations are paused at the moment."

**Why must I confirm my e-mail?**
It proves you can read the inbox. Until then you cannot use the portal, and password reset depends on it. You can type the 6-digit code or press the button in the e-mail.

**How long does the code last?**
10 minutes, with 5 tries. Ask for a new one once a minute.

**Can I change my e-mail address?**
Not yourself. The profile page shows the address but does not let you edit it. Contact the Hub.

**Can I delete my account?**
The member portal has no delete button. Contact the Hub.

**Can staff and members use the same sign-in page?**
Yes. `/sign-in` serves everyone. Staff land in the workspace and members in My account.

## Passwords and security

**I forgot my password.**
Press **Forgot password?** on `/sign-in`. A 6-digit code, or a button, arrives by e-mail. See [Login and authentication](login-and-authentication.md).

**What makes a good password?**
On the live site: at least 12 characters, with upper and lower case letters and a number. Test sites accept 8.

**Why was I locked out?**
After 5 wrong sign-ins from your connection you wait 15 minutes. If 20 wrong attempts happen on one account from anywhere, it is also locked for 15 minutes.

**Will the Hub ever ask for my password or code?**
No. Never share your password or the 6-digit code with anyone, including Hub staff.

**Why was I signed out on my other devices?**
Changing or resetting a password signs out every other device on purpose.

## Programmes and applications

**How do I know a programme is open?**
The badge on the programme page says **Open**. Programmes that have not started say "Opens <date>". See [Browsing the public website](feature-guides/browsing-the-public-website.md).

**Can I save my application and finish later?**
Yes. Press **Save draft**. Drafts are not reviewed. Come back through **My applications**, then **Continue**. A draft can only be finished while the programme is open.

**What files can I upload?**
PDF only. The default limit is 20 MB per file. The limit shown under each upload box is the real one.

**Can I change an application after submitting?**
No. Submitted applications are locked. Send a [message](feature-guides/application-messages.md) to the Hub.

**Can I withdraw?**
Yes, while the status is Submitted or Under review. You cannot apply again to the same programme afterwards. See [Programme applications](feature-guides/programme-applications.md).

**Can I apply to more than one programme?**
Yes. There is one application per programme per person.

**How will I know the result?**
By the bell notification on every status change. An e-mail is sent only when the reviewer chooses to.

**What do the statuses mean?**
Draft, Submitted, Under review, Shortlisted, Accepted, Not selected, Withdrawn. The table is in [Programme applications](feature-guides/programme-applications.md#follow-the-status).

**What is the reference number?**
A code like `UEW-AP-2026-7K3QX9` for an application and `UEW-PR-2026-K4M7QX` for a progress report. Quote it when you write to the Hub.

**Can staff apply?**
No. Staff accounts cannot apply.

## Reports, XP and badges

**When can I send a progress report?**
After an application is Accepted. See [Progress reports](feature-guides/progress-reports.md).

**Can I edit a report?**
Only when the Hub sets it to **Changes requested**.

**What are XP?**
Points for taking part: 10 for an application, 20 for a new report, and more for badges. They do not affect whether you are accepted. See [Badges and leaderboard](feature-guides/badges-and-leaderboard.md).

**How do I appear on the leaderboard?**
Tick **Show me on the public community page and leaderboard** in **Profile & settings**. You also need more than 0 XP.

## E-mail and notifications

**What is the bell?**
In-site notices about your applications, messages, reports and badges. See [Notifications guide](notifications-guide.md).

**Can I stop all e-mail?**
You can stop announcements. Account and application e-mails (codes, confirmations, password changed) are always sent. See [E-mail preferences and unsubscribe](feature-guides/email-preferences-and-unsubscribe.md).

**Why do I get announcements I never asked for?**
The announcement box is ticked by default on the first profile page. Untick it, or press **Unsubscribe** in any announcement.

## Privacy

**Who sees my personal details?**
Reviewers see the details frozen on your application (name, contact details, affiliation, institution and age, among others) and your answers. The reviewer screen leaves out gender and disability. The public sees only what you allow on the community page: name, affiliation, institution, bio, XP and badges. See [Profile and account management](profile-and-account-management.md).

**Which law applies?**
Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), as stated on the forms. Read `/privacy` on the site.
