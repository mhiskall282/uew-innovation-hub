# Member guide

For students, staff, alumni and partners who want to apply to UEW Innovation Hub programmes.

## 1. Create your account

1. Open the Hub website and choose **Create account** (top right).
2. Enter your name, e-mail address, phone number, gender, date of birth and region, then choose a password.
   - You must be at least 15 years old.
   - Your gender and date of birth are used for eligibility and for anonymous inclusion reports to funders. Reviewers never see your gender or disability status.
3. Tick the box to accept the terms and privacy notice, then choose **Create account**.
4. We e-mail you a **6-digit code**. Type it on the next screen. The code works for 10 minutes and you have 5 tries.
   - No e-mail? Check spam, wait a minute, then choose **Send a new code**.
5. Complete your profile: affiliation (student, staff, alumni...), institution, highest education level and the areas you're interested in. We use your interests to recommend programmes.

## 2. Find and apply to a programme

1. Go to **Programmes**. Use the tabs for *Open now*, *Opening soon* and *Closed*, and the chips to filter by type.
2. Open a programme to read what it offers, who can apply and the deadline.
3. Choose **Apply**. Some answers are filled in from your profile (you'll see *Filled from your profile*); you can change them.
4. Use **Save draft** as often as you like. Drafts are not reviewed.
5. When you're ready, tick the declaration and choose **Submit application**. You'll get an e-mail and a notification, and earn **+10 XP**.

After you submit, answers can't be changed. If you made a mistake, message the Hub from the application page.

## 3. Follow your application

Your **Overview** and **My applications** pages show each application's status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Saved, not submitted |
| Submitted | Received, waiting for review |
| Under review | The panel is assessing it |
| Shortlisted | You're through to the next stage; the Hub will contact you |
| Accepted | Congratulations, you've been selected |
| Not selected | Not successful this time |
| Withdrawn | You withdrew it |

The bell icon at the top shows new notifications: status changes, messages from the Hub, report feedback and new badges.

**Messages.** On an application page, scroll to **Messages** to ask the Hub team a question. Replies appear in the same thread and under the bell.

**Withdraw.** While an application is *Submitted* or *Under review* you can withdraw it from its page. This can't be undone.

## 4. If you're accepted: progress and budget reports

Accepted incubatees, grantees and fellows report to the Hub regularly (usually monthly, or when asked).

1. Open **Progress reports** in your account menu, or the accepted application.
2. Choose **New report**.
3. Fill in the period, a summary, the milestones you reached, challenges and next steps, and how many people you've reached.
4. Under **Budget**, enter the total funds received so far, then add a line for each spending item (category, description, budgeted and spent). Totals and your balance are calculated for you. Keep your receipts; the Hub may ask for them.
5. Choose **Submit report** (+20 XP for each new report).

The Hub will mark it **Accepted** or **Changes requested**. If changes are requested, the feedback appears on the report; choose **Make changes**, update it and resubmit.

## 5. Badges, XP and the community page

You earn XP for taking part:

| Action | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Submit an application | 10 |
| Submit a progress report | 20 |
| Accepted into a programme (Participant / Incubatee badge) | 50 |
| Alumni badge | 100 |
| Mentor badge | 120 |
| Finalist badge | 150 |
| Winner badge | 300 |

Levels go from *Newcomer* through *Explorer*, *Builder*, *Innovator* and *Trailblazer* to *Hub Legend*.

To appear on the public **Community** leaderboard, go to **Profile & settings** and tick **Show me on the public community page**. Only your name, affiliation, institution, bio, XP and badges are shown. Untick it at any time to disappear from the page.

## 6. Your account and data

- **Profile & settings**: update your details, interests, announcement e-mails and community visibility, and change your password.
- **Forgot your password?** On the sign-in page choose **Forgot password?**; we e-mail a 6-digit code.
- **Stop announcement e-mails**: use the unsubscribe link in any announcement, or untick it in settings. E-mails about your own applications are always sent.
- Your data is handled under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). See the Privacy page on the website.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The code e-mail never arrives | Check spam/junk. Wait 60 seconds and choose *Send a new code*. Make sure the address on screen is correct. |
| "This code has expired" | Codes last 10 minutes. Ask for a new one. |
| "Too many attempts" | After 5 wrong codes the code is locked. Request a new one. |
| I can't see the Apply button | The programme may not be open yet, may have closed, or may be full. Check the badge on the programme page. |
| "Some answers need attention" | Scroll up; each question with a problem is outlined in red with the reason. |
| My PDF won't upload | Only PDF files are accepted, up to the size shown under the upload box. Export Word documents as PDF first. |
| I can't edit my submitted application | Submitted applications are locked so reviewers assess what you sent. Message the Hub from the application page. |
| I can't create a progress report | Reports open only after an application is *Accepted*. |
| My report says "Changes requested" | Read the feedback on the report, choose *Make changes*, then resubmit. |
| I'm not on the leaderboard | Turn on *Show me on the public community page* in settings. You also need at least some XP. |
| I'm signed out unexpectedly | Sessions end after 2 hours of inactivity. Tick *Keep me signed in* on a personal device. |

Still stuck? Use the **Contact** page and quote your application reference (for example `UEW-AP-2026-7K3QX9`).
